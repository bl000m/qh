Preserve meaning consistently in text, descriptors, properties and relation predicates.
Represent distinct material propositions, including explicit non-events, denials,
unknown assignments, pending approvals and undecided questions; an evidence quote
alone does not count as their extraction. Do not infer missing propositions.
Preserve conditionality and uncertainty throughout every field. A possible outcome,
threshold, expected effect or intention is not an applied/completed/confirmed event.
Retain the distinction between proposed, adopted, withdrawn, suspended and resumed
states, with their source-supported order; suspension does not restore an earlier
proposal. Do not invent transitions or restart dates.
Distinguish the speaker reporting a decision from its explicit actor. Preserve
collective action where stated, individual action where stated, and unknown actors
as unknown. Choosing an alternative does not automatically establish personal rejection.
Attach a supported literal timestamp to the assertion/event it qualifies; isolated
date entities are not a substitute for that linkage. Keep acquisition time separate.
Unknown temporal bounds stay absent/null; do not infer validity intervals from dates.
Cite original evidence for every candidate, including relations. A source quote can
be genuine while the interpretation is wrong; do not promote a claim on that basis.
Keep the existing envelope and open descriptor vocabulary. All output remains proposed;
descriptor assignment certainty is distinct from factual truth and human validation.
